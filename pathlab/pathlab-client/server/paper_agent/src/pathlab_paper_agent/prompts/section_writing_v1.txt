Task: Complete the manuscript body by writing every requested LaTeX section in one call. This is adapted from PaperOrchestra Appendix F.4, but PathLab adds deterministic fact locks and renderer placeholders.

Critical PathLab contract:
1. Return ONLY valid JSON. The top-level object must contain a `sections` object whose keys are the requested section slugs and whose values are LaTeX strings.
2. Generate all sections listed in `section_contract.required_section_slugs`, including introduction and related_work. Write final text for `drafts/sections/`; do not write to `drafts/intro_rw_draft/`.
3. Treat `drafts/intro_rw_draft` as source material. You may polish style, reorder paragraphs, and align it with Methods/Results/Discussion, but you must preserve every required citation key.
4. Citation syntax is mandatory: use `[CITE:paperId]` only. Do not output raw `\cite{}` or any citation key outside `section_contract.allowed_cite_keys`.
5. Metric syntax is mandatory: use `{{m:metric_key}}` for any metric value. Do not directly write metric numbers, percentages, p-values, confidence intervals, or decimals.
6. Dataset, model, experimental setup, and results facts must come from locked blocks. Use `\input{locked_blocks/...}` in Methods/Experiments/Results instead of rewriting those facts.
7. Every ready figure in `figures_manifest.figures` must appear in the final sections with both its manifest `figure_id` and its relative `path` in `\includegraphics{...}`. Tables may only be included from `section_contract.table_paths`; do not invent files.
8. Do not leak task IDs, case IDs, patient IDs, raw file paths, email addresses, real names, or unsanitized institution names.
9. Do not introduce experimental claims beyond outline, experimental_log, locked_blocks, figures_manifest, tables, and citations.
10. Keep prose concise, technical, and venue-aware. Captions in figures_manifest are factual; do not expand them into unsupported narratives.
11. Write for `section_contract.generation_template_id` as the target venue. Follow `section_guidelines[section_slug]` as the section-by-section writing contract whenever it is present.
